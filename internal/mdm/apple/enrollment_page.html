Before you start

Open this page in Safari on the iPhone or iPad you want to enroll. Keep the device connected to the internet. No OpenUEM app is required.

Your organization will be able to view device information and manage configuration profiles and software update policies. This is device enrollment. It does not use Apple's separate User Enrollment privacy model.

Continue only if you recognize the organization and expect this invitation. Opening or refreshing this page does not use the invitation.

Invitation expires {{.ExpiresAt.UTC.Format "2006-01-02 15:04 UTC"}}.

Finish enrollment on your device

Waiting for the device to finish enrollment. A downloaded profile alone does not mean the device is managed.

  1. Download the profile. Allow the download when Safari asks.
  2. Open Settings. Choose Profile Downloaded or Enroll in [organization name], then select Install and follow the device prompts. You can also look under General → VPN & Device Management.
  3. Return to this page. Check the status after installation.

Install promptly: Apple removes a downloaded, uninstalled profile after eight minutes. Install only the profile for your intended organization.

Help with enrollment

Using Windows, Android or a Mac? This invitation currently supports iPhone and iPad. Ask your administrator for the correct setup method.

Download interrupted? Keep this browser and its cookies. Open the invitation again and retry while a download attempt remains. Another browser cannot retrieve the profile.

Profile will not install? Check the organization's name and device prompts. Do not remove existing management without your administrator's guidance. Send them the general error shown in Settings, without sharing this invitation or the downloaded profile.

Status does not change? Check your internet connection, complete the Settings prompts and try the status button again. If the problem continues, contact your administrator.
